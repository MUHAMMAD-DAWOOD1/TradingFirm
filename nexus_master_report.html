5. Tailored Capital Sizing & Risk Management (Aapka Paisa, Aapka Risk)

Platform par analysis run karte waqt trader apna Account Capital ($) aur Risk % select karta hai (presets: $1k, $5k, $10k, $25k, $50k, $100k). System exact mathematical sizing calculate karta hai:

Position Size (Lots) = (Account Capital × Risk %) / |Optimal Entry - Stop Loss|

Exact Lot Size Calculation

Maslan $10,000 capital aur 2% risk par Gold ke liye exact 0.05 Lots (5.11 units) calculate hota hai taake account kabhi overexpose na ho.

Maximum Dollar Loss (SL)

Projected Profit (TP1 & TP2)
